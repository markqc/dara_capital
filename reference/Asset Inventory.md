# DARA Capital asset inventory

The reference is a flattened six-screen PNG preview. The recreation uses matched typography and a reconstructed palette. Original font files, brand colors, photography, and vector artwork were unavailable.

## Typography

| Element | Typeface in recreation | Appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero and section headings, metrics, quote | Baskerville Regular | High-contrast serif |
| Body, navigation, cards, forms, dashboard | Arial Regular | Neutral sans serif |
| Eyebrows, buttons, footer labels | Arial Bold | Compact, mostly uppercase |
| DAC / DARA CAPITAL | Redrawn lettermark and spaced Arial Bold caption | Approximation of the preview logo |

## Artwork generation

Built-in image generation was used for the two artwork sets.

**Hero artwork prompt**

> Create a clean 2-column by 2-row contact sheet of FOUR distinct edge-to-edge rectangular photorealistic website hero images for a luxury finance brand, all black with deep crimson red and subtle white highlights. Exactly equal quadrants, no gutters, no text, no letters, no logos. Upper left: glowing red wireframe earth with pinpoints over dark financial candlestick bars, red trend lines, black background. Upper right: modern black marble reception desk in moody corporate lobby with large windows and red LED accent strips. Lower left: close-up sweeping curved black glass office architecture with dramatic continuous saturated red facade light strips. Lower right: close-up dark photoreal earth hemisphere from space centered on Eurasia with red illuminated networks and subtle vertical stock chart marks on black. Preserve separate compositions in each quadrant. Crisp realistic dark editorial campaign art.

**Article artwork prompt**

> Exactly equal 2x2 contact sheet with no gutters of four dark editorial finance website article thumbnails, no text or logos. Upper left: realistic dense modern city skyline at dusk, restrained blue-gray and slate highlights. Upper right: vivid red glowing 3D human brain hologram against near-black background. Lower left: elegant blue luminous network graph with simple fintech symbols such as lock, window, credit card, and chart, near-black background. Lower right: flat illuminated deep crimson world map and fine geographic network lights on black. Clean subject centered separately in each of the four quadrants.
